WHAT THE CONTROLLER ACTUALLY SEES AND DOES

Camera-derived features, public instrument readings and a goal card go in; a choice of handle A or B comes out. The simulator executes the pull. The controller is not given hidden wiring, ground-truth object coordinates or the correct handle at test time.

不是纯像素到鼠标。模型读相机特征、公开仪表和目标卡，选 A 或 B；由模拟器执行。只有人工模式拖动鼠标。训练有动作示范，测试不提供答案。

Reading the films / 怎么看

The teal outlines are the requested berths. Either handle moves all four cabins; only three are scored. Two hits pass a card. The two choices are locked before either stroke is executed; both cards must pass for a pair to count.

青框是目标，不是预测。第一次试拉后复位；两张卡都选号才执行，都从同一起点出发。每张卡三轨中对两轨即过；整对计分须两张都过。
